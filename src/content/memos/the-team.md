We are a seasoned attorney and former in-house leader who understands the business of law and what your clients want, a former C-suite executive who knows how to grow firm profits, and a senior engineer who has built technology for Amazon and Blue Origin.

**[Shawn Liu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/shawn-liu-97069014/)** was Facebook’s 4th product counsel, the second lawyer at the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative (helping grow the team from 3 to 20 people), and the Legal Director at Breakthrough Energy, a cleantech funding platform founded by Bill Gates. Before working in-house, he litigated at Gibson Dunn, working on teams that were victorious at the Supreme Court (*Alice Corp. v. CLS Bank*), the Ninth Circuit (*Quijada-Aguilar v. Lynch*), and trial courts (*Realtime Data v. T-Mobile*, in E.D. Texas). He holds an AB from Harvard College with a concentration in Chemistry and Physics, a JD from the University of Chicago, and clerked for Judge Sandra Ikuta on the Ninth Circuit.

**[Uday Khedkar](https://www.linkedin.com/in/uday-khedkar-58bb716/)** is a business leader with over 25 years of general management experience across large corporations and entrepreneurial mid-size companies. At USG Corporation, he worked in its New Business Ventures Incubator and led the company’s entry into consumer paint. He then served as CFO of Feldco, a family-backed group of Midwest companies with over $100 million in combined revenue. He later led the turnaround of one of its acquisitions, Danley’s Garages, where he served as President. Uday holds an undergraduate degree in accounting from the University of Illinois at Chicago and an MBA from the Kellogg School of Management at Northwestern University.

**[Mickey Muldoon](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mickeymuldoon/)** is a technologist who has spent the past decade building high-performance enterprise software systems for global freight logistics (Amazon) and reusable rocket manufacturing (Blue Origin). He holds an AB from Harvard College with a concentration in Psychology and an MA in Computer Science from Brooklyn College.
